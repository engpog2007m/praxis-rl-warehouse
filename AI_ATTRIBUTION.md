# AI Assistance Disclosure

This disclosure is made under The George Washington University policy on the use of
generative AI, which permits AI assistance with code provided it is attributed and the
author can explain the code.

## Tool

Claude (Anthropic), used through the claude.ai interface, 2026.

## Scope of assistance

Claude assisted with code development, including:

- simulation environments (`src/simulation/`), reward functions and observation design
- training, evaluation and data-harvesting scripts (`scripts/`)
- diagnosis of defects, including the wheel-traction physics defect and odometry
  divergence documented in `docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`
- statistical analysis code and figure generation
- the repository tooling in `tools/`, `analysis/`, `tests/` and `run_all.sh`
- repository documentation in `README.md` and `docs/`

## Author's role

The author defined the research questions and hypotheses, selected the methods,
directed all development, ran every experiment on the author's own hardware, verified
results, and made all research decisions, including the decision to report the Era 1
physics defect and the partial support for H3.
